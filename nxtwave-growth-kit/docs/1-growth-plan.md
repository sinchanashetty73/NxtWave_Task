# Growth Plan: 500 Final-Year Registrations in 7 Days
**Workshop:** "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes" · **Budget:** ₹2,000 · **Author:** Sinchana Shetty

---

## 1. Who I'm targeting

**Primary persona: "Placement-anxious Priya/Ravi"**: a final-year engineering student (CSE/IT/AIML first, ECE/EEE second) at a tier-2 or tier-3 college, in the middle of campus placement season.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they feel** | "Everyone says AI, I have no project to show. I'm late." |
| **What they've tried** | YouTube playlists, half-finished courses, copy-paste mini-projects |
| **Biggest fear** | Being asked "what have you built?" in an interview and having nothing real |
| **Where they live** | College WhatsApp groups, club/department groups, Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram |
| **Who they trust** | Classmates and seniors, not ads |

**Why they'd care:** a project is the one thing that moves interview outcomes, and this promises one in an hour, for free.

**What makes them register (4 triggers):**
1. **Concrete outcome**: "leave with a working project + GitHub link + a resume line", not "learn AI".
2. **Low risk**: free, 60 minutes, no experience needed.
3. **Personal relevance**: the project picker shows *their* branch and *their* topic.
4. **Friends**: a friend sent it and a friend is going.

---

## 2. Campaign: 4 channels, no more

Insight: this audience registers when **a peer sends it in a group they already read**. So paid ads get the smallest share of budget and peer distribution gets most of the effort.

| # | Channel | What I do | Why it works | Regs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WhatsApp via campus ambassadors** | Recruit 30 final-years (class reps, club leads) via LinkedIn/Instagram DMs. Give each a ready message, poster and a personal tracked link. They post in ~3 groups each. | Trusted sender, group the student already reads, zero cost | **170** |
| 2 | **Email to clubs, TPOs and HODs** | AI-personalised email to 200 coding/AI/IEEE clubs and placement cells using public contact pages. Ask for one forward to their student group. Two waves (Day 2, Day 5). | Faculty/club forwards carry authority; ~20% respond | **90** |
| 3 | **Referral loop (built into landing page)** | Every registrant gets a personal link + share card. Unlocks: 1 friend = Starter Kit, 3 = Interview Cheat-Sheet, 5 = priority Q&A, top 5 = ₹100 voucher. | Turns each signup into a distributor; the tiers reward small effort | **110** |
| 4 | **Social media (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn)** | 5 short reels (finished project demo, "pick your project", rewards reveal, before/after resume) made with the Poster Studio + a content pack; ambassadors and campus pages repost; boost the best reel with ₹1,000. | Where students already scroll; reels show the outcome and the rewards in 15 seconds | **130** |
| | | | **Total** | **500** |

### 7-day calendar
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Finish page, tracker, posters. Recruit ambassadors. Seed 20 registrations from friends/network. Story teaser. |
| 2 | Ambassadors go live. Email wave 1. **Reel 1** + LinkedIn post. |
| 3 | Nudge #1 in groups. Share first leaderboard. Check dashboard: which source leads? |
| 4 | **Reel 2**; boost the best performer at ₹1,000. Telegram groups. |
| 5 | Email wave 2. **Reel 3 (rewards)**. Referral push to existing registrants. |
| 6 | **Reel 4**. DMs to ambassadors who are behind. "Last 48 hours" status. |
| 7 | **Reel 5**, final call, reminder with joining link to all registrants. |

### Math (so 500 is not a wish)
- 500 registrations ÷ ~30% visit-to-register (warm, peer-sent traffic) ≈ **1,650 visits** needed.
- Ambassadors: 30 × 3 groups × ~60 members ≈ 5,400 views → ~3% register ≈ 170.
- Emails: 200 sent → ~28 forwards × ~80-member groups → ≈ 90.
- Referrals: ~30% of the first ~390 share and bring ~1 each ≈ 110.
- Social: ~15,000 organic views × ~0.5% register ≈ 75, plus the ₹1,000 boost (~140 clicks at ~₹7, ~35% register ≈ 50) ≈ 125–130. These are estimates; reach varies a lot.
- If a channel runs 30% short, the dashboard shows it by Day 3 and I move effort to the best performer.

### Budget: ₹2,000
| Item | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Top-5 referral vouchers (₹100 each) | 500 |
| Boost the best-performing reel (Day 4–5) | 1,000 |
| Buffer (domain / tools / replacement vouchers) | 500 |

Everything else is free: AI for copy and personalisation, Canva free, GitHub Pages for hosting.

### How I'll know it's working (checked daily in the dashboard)
Registrations per day, source mix, referral share, projected finish. Target by end of Day 3: 150+. If below 100, I cut the weakest channel and re-push the strongest.

## 3. What I built
A **landing page + referral engine + control-room dashboard** (see `/landing`). Highlights: a lab-themed "Builder ID" sign-up where each student gets a codename and class, personal referral link, ranks that unlock rewards (Recruit → Pioneer), downloadable share card, branch-specific project picker, live seat meter and leaderboard, and a dashboard that tracks channel performance and projects whether 500 will be hit. Plus a **Poster & Caption Studio** (`/social`) that makes post/story/LinkedIn graphics with the rewards strip, and per-platform captions with tracked links.

# 3-Minute Video Script

Tip: screen-record the landing page and admin dashboard while you talk. Use your own words; read this as a guide, not a script.

**0:00–0:25 · Who and why**
"Hi, I'm Sinchana, a final-year AI and ML student. When I read this challenge I asked one question: why would a final-year student give up an evening for a free workshop? The answer I landed on is placement anxiety. Everyone's asked 'what have you built?', and most students don't have a good answer."

**0:25–1:05 · The plan**
*(show the plan doc)*
"I picked four channels and ignored the rest. Campus ambassadors in WhatsApp groups bring about 170. Emails to clubs and placement cells bring about 90. A referral loop built into the page brings about 110. And short-form social, Instagram reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status, with a ₹1,000 boost on the best reel, brings about 130. That's 500. I split the ₹2,000 between referral vouchers, one boost and a buffer. Students trust friends, not ads, so most of the effort goes to peer distribution."

**1:05–2:15 · What I built**
*(screen: landing page)*
"Everyone builds a landing page, so I built a growth loop on top of it. First, the project picker: pick your branch and interest and you see the exact project you'd build. Then registration. *(register live)* *(optional: flash the Poster Studio making a reel cover with the rewards strip)* Straight away you get a personal referral link, reward tiers that unlock as friends join, and a share card with your name that you can post. *(download the card)* Referrals are tracked, and there's a leaderboard."

*(screen: admin dashboard, load demo data)*
"Behind it is a control room. It shows registrations per day, which channel is working, referral share, and a projection of whether we'll hit 500. If we're behind on Day 3, I know which channel to push."

**2:15–2:45 · How I used AI, and what I rejected**
"I used AI for the page code, the email personalisation and the message copy. It also suggested things I rejected: fake 'only 12 seats left' urgency, because there's no seat limit and it would damage trust, and buying scraped phone numbers for a broadcast, because it's spammy and converts badly."

**2:45–3:00 · Close**
"With 24 more hours I'd connect a real Google Sheet, add WhatsApp reminders and build a project-evaluation step for after the workshop. Thanks for watching."

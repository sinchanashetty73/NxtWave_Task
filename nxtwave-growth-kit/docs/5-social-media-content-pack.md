# Social Media Content Pack

Goal: make the workshop show up where final-year students already scroll, and give them a reason to register *now*: **rewards + a project they can show in interviews**.

## 1. Where to post (ranked by where students spend time)

| Rank | Platform | Why it's here | Role in the campaign |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WhatsApp Status + groups** | Students check it many times a day; a classmate's status is trusted | Main conversion driver (ambassadors repost the poster) |
| 2 | **Instagram Reels + Stories** | Biggest short-video habit for this age group; reels can be discovered by strangers | Reach + the "wow" demo clip |
| 3 | **YouTube Shorts** | Students already search "AI project for final year" | Evergreen reach, also catches search traffic |
| 4 | **LinkedIn** | Placement-minded audience, seniors and recruiters watch | Credibility; posts by ambassadors and by you |
| 5 | **Telegram placement-prep groups** | Many college and prep groups already live here | Cheap extra reach, one message each |

Rankings are my judgment, not measured data. The dashboard tells you the real winner by Day 3 (every link below carries a source tag).

## 3. The hook: what makes people stop and register
Every post leads with one of these (they're all true, nothing fake):

1. "Placements in 3 months. Zero AI projects on your resume?"
2. "Build a working AI project in 60 minutes. Free."
3. "'What have you built?' Every interviewer asks it. Be ready."
4. "Register + invite friends and unlock: Starter Kit, Interview Cheat-Sheet, priority Q&A."

**Reward line to put on every poster/caption** (only promise what you can deliver):
> Register free → get the workshop recording. Invite friends → unlock the AI Project Starter Kit (1), Interview Cheat-Sheet (3), priority Q&A (5). Top 5 referrers win ₹100.

## 4. Five pieces of content (all makeable free with AI + Canva/CapCut)

### Reel 1: "The question" (15–20 s)
- **Shot 1 (0–3s):** Text on screen: *"Interviewer: What have you built?"* Quick cut to a blank, panicked face.
- **Shot 2 (3–10s):** Screen recording of the finished AI project working.
- **Shot 3 (10–17s):** "I built this in 60 minutes. Free workshop, link in bio."
- **CTA overlay:** "Register + invite friends = rewards."

### Reel 2: "Pick your project" (20 s)
Screen-record the project picker on the landing page. Voiceover: *"Choose your branch, choose what you care about, see the exact project you'd build."* End on the register button.

### Reel 3: "Rewards reveal" (15 s)
Show the reward tiers filling up as friends register (screen-record the page). Text: *"1 friend = Starter Kit. 3 = Interview Cheat-Sheet. 5 = priority Q&A."* Hook: *"Free stuff for sharing with your roommates."*

### Reel 4: "Before / after" (20 s)
Split screen: left = a resume with an empty Projects section, right = same resume with an AI project and GitHub link. Caption: *"60 minutes between these two."*

### Reel 5: "Last 48 hours" (12 s)
Fast text-only reel with the real registration count (take it from the dashboard) and the workshop time. Honest urgency only: the date is real, and don't claim limited seats.

**Where each goes:** Reels 1–5 on Instagram and YouTube Shorts; Reels 1 and 4 also as LinkedIn native video; all five as 30-second WhatsApp Status clips via ambassadors.

## 7. Partnerships that cost ₹0
- Ask 10–15 **college tech/meme/campus Instagram pages** and **student club accounts** to post the poster. Offer a shout-out and early access to the Starter Kit in return.
- Ask each ambassador to put the poster on their own Status and **tag you**, then reshare their Status screenshots in groups.

## 8. Honest expectations
Reach is unpredictable; one reel may flop and another may take off. That's why the plan puts only ~130 of 500 registrations on social, and why each post carries its own tracked link, so by Day 3 you can double down on what's working.

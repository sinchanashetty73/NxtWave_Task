// 08 · faq

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No fees, no card details. We use free tools only.

I'm not from CSE. Can I join?

Yes. The project is built to be followed by anyone, any branch. Use the experiment picker above to see your version.

What if I can't attend live?

Register anyway. Registered students get the recording.

Do I need to know coding?

Basic familiarity helps, but AI writes most of the code. We'll teach you to direct it.

How do codenames and ranks work?

You get a codename when you register (re-roll it until you like it). Your rank goes up as friends register with your link: Explorer at 1, Navigator at 3, Commander at 5, Legend if you finish in the top 5.
